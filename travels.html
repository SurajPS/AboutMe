My Travel Page

This is something that I enjoy doing - travelling. Over the last couple of years, I have travelled to different places mostly in Europe. My partner in crime for this fun activity has always been my supportive wife. Living in Germany has been really great until now. I have lived close to the German-Swiss-Austrian border for the last 6.5 years and have seemed to like it. Most of my travel has been in the 500km radius from my place of stay. I would like to showcase my travel outside this 500km sphere here. Probably in the near future, I shall ramp up the places around Bodensee Region


Croatia

One big Travel of 2023 was the trip to Croatia- which was more of a Road trip. It was an amazing drive all the way from the Croatian Capital, Zagreb, to Game of Thrones' Kings Landing, Dubrovnik, and all the way back. The coastal drive one week trip was really interesting filled with laughter, excitement and relaxation

Norway

The biggest trip of 2024 has to definitely be the luckiest tripm where the northern lights were witnessed. Chasing the lights is a phenomenon that one has to go through in Norway, but to get the polar lights on the first go? That definitely is another thing. The same gagn as of Croatia 2023 chased the lights with us also doing other activities like snow biking , hiking and definitely chilling.

French Riviera

One of my Old trips involved a beautiful visit to the French Riviera, a beautiful coast line filled with amazing sea food and wonderful people. Wonderful activities like hiking and attending the carneval marked this trip to a very memorable one


Tenerife

This has always been my personal favourite, and I still do not know why. Its by far the best trip i have ever had. Maybe its just the fact that the vacation was inbetween the atlantic ocean on an island with all sort of terrains. Tenerife is by far one of the most beautiful places that I have visited.
